Seventy-nine years on Delisle Road

The mills went. The shop stayed.

Since 1947 one family has made mithai here. The trays change with the season — mawa through the cold months, dry fruit through the wedding months — but the method has not. Everything on the counter is made by us, in our own kitchen, and it is meant to be eaten the same day.
