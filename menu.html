Allergen note. Our sweets contain dairy (whole milk, khoya, mawa, pure ghee) and tree nuts (Goan cashews, Californian almonds, green pistachios, Afghan figs), and many are finished with genuine edible silver varq. Some savoury items contain wheat. Everything is crafted in one kitchen that handles nuts and dairy throughout — cross-contact cannot be ruled out. Please inform our staff of any dietary constraints when ordering.
